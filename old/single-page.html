Choose a colony

Find a coral you can recognise and, ideally, revisit. Record the location and date. Observe without touching or disturbing the colony.

02 /

Capture it in 3D

Move slowly around the coral while recording an underwater video. Capture overlapping views, then use a reconstruction app to create a model.

Share—and return

Share your model and observations with the project. Return to the same colony to document its growth and change. A single scan is valuable too.

Our approach

Look closer.
Come back.
See what changes.

A coral colony is a living structure. A three-dimensional record lets us explore its surface and revisit details beyond a single photograph.

Adopt-a-Coral brings together divers, researchers, and local communities to document individual colonies. Repeated scans can support research into coral growth, partial mortality, recovery, and changes in colony form.

Built around local participation

We welcome dive centres, conservation groups, and independent contributors. The aim is a useful, shared record that grows through continued observation.
